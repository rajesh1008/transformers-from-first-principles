Chapter 9 scripts, run from 04-code/ch09 (NumPy only, plus the `regex` module for _bpe.py). GPT-2 scripts need
Chapter 8's download of GPT-2 small in ~/.cache/tfp/gpt2/ (model.safetensors, vocab.json) and merges.txt in the same folder.
Order used for the book:
one_vs_two.py  hand_two_heads.py  blocks.py  split_tiny.py  how_many.py  rotary_heads.py  induction_toy.py
gpt2_circuits.py (about 5 minutes)  heads_gallery.py (needs ch08/outputs/gpt2_shares.npy)  gpt2_prune.py (about 6 minutes)
lab_multihead.py  usage.py  scoreboard.py  solutions.py
Helpers: _common.py (toy rows, hand tables, seed 1865, recorded timings), _gpt2run.py (GPT-2 small in NumPy with
head removal: zero, mean, naive), _bpe.py (GPT-2 byte-level BPE from vocab.json and merges.txt).
Outputs are saved in outputs/ (python3 X.py > outputs/X.txt). Timings are recorded once in outputs/*.json;
pass --measure to how_many.py or scoreboard.py to time your own machine (how_many.py with 128 heads needs about 4 GB free).
Last full run: 10 November 2026, book build machine (CPU), NumPy 2.5.4.
Figures: python3 03-figures/src/ch09_figs.py from the project root (reads the outputs above).
