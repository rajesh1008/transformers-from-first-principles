Things that went wrong or changed on the way, and are told in the chapter:
  - The sweep was first run with one reading order and looked decided (1e-3 best).  A second order (added after the first results)
    made 2e-3 best; a third order at the three lowest rates put them within 0.07.  The chapter reports all three.
  - The first explanation of the WSD-cosine gap (cosine stops at 0.1 of the peak) was tested with a cosine to zero, which did worse.
  - The trunk was held-out-evaluated every 100 steps, so "trunk at the branch's end" uses the last mark before it (600, 1,200).
  - Weight decay (WD), the cosine floor (FLOOR) and the reading order (ORDER) are environment variables added to train_tiny.py
    during the tick; the runs that used them are in jobs_extra.txt and jobs_orders.txt.
