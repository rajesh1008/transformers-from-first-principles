Chapter 12 scripts, run from 04-code/ch12 (NumPy only; matplotlib for figures). Order used for the book:
hand_ffn.py  activations.py  params.py  tiny_ffn.py  gpt2_neurons.py  gpt2_bracket.py  lab12.py  solutions.py
Helpers: _common.py (toy rows, hand tables, seed 1865; adds ../ch09 to the path for _gpt2run.py and _bpe.py).
New Tiny module: ../tiny/ffn.py (FeedForward, plain or gated; relu, gelu, gelu_exact, silu).
GPT-2 scripts read the GPT-2 small weights prepared in Chapter 9 and the two public-domain novels in ../data.
gpt2_neurons.py takes about 5 minutes and solutions.py (Exercise 9) longer on the book's CPU.
Outputs are saved in outputs/ (python3 X.py > outputs/X.txt). tiny_ffn.py records its timings in
outputs/tiny_ffn_timing.json; gpt2_bracket.py also writes outputs/bracket_act.npy for Figure 12.4.
Figures: python3 ../../03-figures/src/ch12_figs.py (from the project root: python3 03-figures/src/ch12_figs.py)
Last full run: 10 November 2026, book build machine (CPU, Intel Xeon, 8 cores), NumPy 2.5.4.
